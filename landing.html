Why We Built This

THE INDUSTRY'S BIGGEST PROBLEM

Construction estimating hasn't changed in decades. It is still done by hand — slow, expensive, and riddled with errors that kill margins and lose bids.

⏱️

Days Lost on Takeoffs

A single blueprint can take a quantity surveyor 2–5 days to price manually. Miss a revision and you do it all again. Deadlines are lost before the bid is even written.

📉

Human Error Kills Margins

Industry studies show up to 35% of construction projects run over budget — often traced back to errors made at the estimate stage. A bad takeoff costs more than the software ever would.

💸

QS Firms Are Expensive

Hiring a quantity surveyor costs $80–$200/hr. For SMEs and independent contractors, that is not an option. Projects either go unestimated or bids are guessed — both ways you lose.

⏱️

Turnaround That Wins Bids

A single plan can take a QS two to five days to price by hand, and a revision sends it back to the start. ForgeEstimate returns a line-itemed takeoff in under a minute, so pricing starts the same day the drawings land.
